# 02. User Personas & Enterprise Journeys

## 1. Primary User Personas

### Persona A: Ramesh Kumar — Micro Kirana Owner (Level 0 -> Level 1)
- **Age**: 46 | **Location**: Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh
- **Business**: Traditional corner grocery store, 15 years in operation, Rs 2.5 Lakh monthly turnover.
- **Hardware**: Personal 4G Android smartphone; no computer or dedicated POS.
- **Pain Points**: Credit (Udhaar) disputes, cash leakage, long checkout lines during evening rush.
- **DigiMitra Journey**:
  1. Registers via phone in Hindi.
  2. Takes 14-question C3 assessment in 4 minutes.
  3. Diagnosed as **Level 0 (Unconnected)** due to lack of dedicated device and paper-only books.
  4. Receives Phase 1 roadmap: Adopt UPI QR soundbox, separate personal and business SIMs, install digital ledger (Khatabook/Vyapar).
  5. Reviews Learning Flashcard on "Dedicated Business Smartphone" and "Digital Udhaar Khata".
  6. Successfully transitions to **Level 1 (Basic)** within 4 weeks.

### Persona B: Sunita Devi — Small Garment Manufacturer (Level 1 -> Level 2)
- **Age**: 38 | **Location**: Surat, Gujarat
- **Business**: Apparel stitching unit, 12 workers, Rs 12 Lakh monthly turnover.
- **Hardware**: Shared desktop computer, 2 smartphones, broadband connection.
- **Pain Points**: Fabric inventory stockouts, delayed customer payments, lack of formal GST invoices.
- **DigiMitra Journey**:
  1. Sets budget tier to Rs 2,500/month.
  2. Diagnosed as **Level 1 (Basic)**.
  3. Receives Phase 2 roadmap: Implement barcode inventory tagging, computerized GST billing, and TReDS enrollment for bill discounting.
  4. Learns about **MSME ZED Certification** and **TReDS** through Government Initiatives tab.
  5. Unlocks **Level 2 (Standardized)** capabilities.

### Persona C: Vikram Joshi — Medium Precision Tooling Enterprise (Level 2 -> Level 3)
- **Age**: 51 | **Location**: Pune, Maharashtra
- **Business**: Automotive component manufacturer, 45 employees, Rs 85 Lakh monthly turnover.
- **Hardware**: LAN, 8 desktop workstations, Tally Prime, biometric attendance.
- **Pain Points**: Departmental data silos, manual production scheduling, lack of executive dashboards.
- **DigiMitra Journey**:
  1. Diagnosed as **Level 2 (Standardized)**.
  2. Receives Level 3 roadmap: Multi-channel customer ordering, Power BI sales analytics, automated backup, and GeM portal listing.
  3. Accesses Level 3 Flashcards on "Advanced Analytics & Dashboards" and "Cybersecurity & 3-2-1 Cloud Backups".
