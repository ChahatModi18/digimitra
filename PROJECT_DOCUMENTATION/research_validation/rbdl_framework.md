# RBDL Framework: Requirement-Based Digitalization Levels

The **RBDL Framework** classifies Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) in India into four progressive maturity tiers:

| Level | Name | Primary Characteristics | Target Interventions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Level 0** | Non-Digital / Traditional Paper-Based | Cash-only transactions, physical ledger (Bahi Khata), zero web presence. | Digital payments (UPI QR), Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business. |
| **Level 1** | Basic Digital Presence & Payments | Active UPI/QR payments, basic local listings, smartphone customer communication. | Digital billing/bookkeeping software (Vyapar, Tally), digital cataloging. |
| **Level 2** | Operational & Process Digitalization | Computerized accounting/invoicing, structured inventory control, social commerce/ONDC. | CRM integration, automated stock notifications, basic data analytics. |
| **Level 3** | Integrated & Data-Driven Digital Enterprise | Unified Cloud ERP/CRM, multi-channel automated order fulfillment, predictive insights. | Advanced demand forecasting, loyalty automation, business intelligence. |
