# DigiMitra Research Datasets Directory

This directory stores anonymized, versioned assessment snapshots, rule evaluation traces, and empirical survey results for MSME digitalization research in India.

## Data Governance & Compliance
- Direct identifiers (Owner name, contact numbers, exact street address) are stripped in accordance with DPDP Act (2023) privacy guidelines.
- Dataset exports maintain immutable hash references to the corresponding Question Bank version and Rule Engine version.
